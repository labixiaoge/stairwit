A new English word for the perfect comeback that arrives too late, entered in make.fun's "Invent a Word We Need" bounty ($5 in USDC).

## The word

> stairwit: the perfect comeback you think of only after the argument is over.

*Stair* + *wit*: the wit of the staircase. Diderot named the feeling *l'esprit de l'escalier* — the devastating reply that comes to mind only once you are already on the stairs, leaving. English never grew a single word for it, so this one does: sayable, spellable, and instantly recognisable to anyone who has ever lost an argument in the room and won it in the hallway.
